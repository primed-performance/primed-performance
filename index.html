How to work with us

Your coaching journey, step by step

Structured, evidence-based coaching tailored to your sport and goals, with Em in your corner from day one.

Step 1

Expression of interest

Complete the form so we can check we're the right fit for each other. Coach Em will personally be in touch within 24 to 48 hours.

Step 2

Initial set up meeting

We review your training load, competition schedule and goals, pinpoint the gaps and map out your road to success.

Step 3

Performance assessment

You complete a targeted assessment based on your sport and goals. These measures provide the objective data that informs your program design.

Step 4

Program delivery

Your individualised program is delivered through TrainHeroic, with load management, video demos, coaching cues and progress tracking. Programs run a minimum of 12 weeks to ensure real progression.

Step 5

Coaching & execution

This is not a program-only service. You engage directly with a university-qualified exercise and sport scientist, with weekly check-ins and video feedback.

Step 6

Review & progress

Depending on your membership level, we connect monthly or fortnightly to review progress, refine programming and plan upcoming phases for long-term development.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do you monitor technique and ensure I'm lifting or moving correctly?

Coaching goes far beyond providing a program. Technique is monitored through structured video analysis, where you submit footage for review and feedback. Video is one of the most effective tools for performance improvement, self-awareness and long-term development.

Do I have to be "elite" to work with you?

No, you don't need to be competing at an elite level. But you do need an elite attitude. Working with a coach requires commitment, consistency and ownership. We work with athletes who are ready to show up, apply the process and make meaningful change.

What makes you different to an online personal trainer?

This service focuses on long-term athlete development, performance outcomes and evidence-based decision-making. Training is planned around your sport, competition calendar and progression needs, with ongoing assessment rather than static programs.

What is an exercise & sport scientist?

A university-qualified professional trained in human movement, physiology, biomechanics, performance analysis and training prescription. At Primed Performance, every decision is guided by evidence, data and the specific demands of your sport, not trends or guesswork.

Why do I have to pay for the set up session?

The set-up session is a strategy session, not a discovery call. Your enquiry form already provides foundational information, so we come prepared with direction. During the session we assess your current training load, clarify priorities and map out a clear plan.

Why do I have to commit for 12 weeks?

Meaningful change takes time and consistency. Strength, resilience and performance development require structured progression. A 12-week commitment ensures you fully engage in the process, build momentum and experience real, sustainable improvement.
