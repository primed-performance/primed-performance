FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does online coaching even work?

It's simpler than it sounds. After your free discovery call and an initial assessment, I build your program and it lands in the TrainHeroic app on your phone. Every session is laid out for you with video demos, coaching cues, sets, reps and loads. You train when and where it suits you, log how it went, and I use that to adjust what comes next. On 1:1 coaching you can message me any time, and we catch up over video every month (fortnightly on Elite) to review your progress and plan your next block.

How do I know I'm doing the right technique when you're not there?

Every exercise in your program comes with a video demo and coaching cues, so you know what good looks like before you start. Then you film your sets on your phone and send them through. In return you get an in-depth video from me evaluating your movement, explaining any technical changes you need to make and how the movement should feel when you get it right. We also go through your technique in our video meetings, so we can make sure everything is going smoothly. If a movement isn't clicking, I'll swap it for one that suits your body better while we build it up. Video feedback and video meetings are part of Perform and Elite. On Build, use your monthly check-in to flag anything that doesn't feel right.

Do I have to be "elite" to work with you?

No, you don't need to be competing at an elite level. But you do need an elite attitude. Working with a coach requires commitment, consistency and ownership. We work with people who are ready to show up, apply the process and make meaningful change.

What makes you different to an online personal trainer?

This service focuses on long-term development, performance outcomes and evidence-based decision-making. Training is planned around your goals, your calendar and your progression needs, with ongoing assessment rather than static programs.

What is an exercise & sport scientist?

A university-qualified professional trained in human movement, physiology, biomechanics, performance analysis and training prescription. At Primed Performance, every decision is guided by evidence, data and the specific demands of your sport, not trends or guesswork.

Is the first call free?

Yes. Your first step is a free 15 minute discovery call. Your enquiry form gives me the basics, so we use the call to talk through your goals and training and work out the best option for you. No cost, no obligation.

Why do I have to commit for 12 weeks?

Meaningful change takes time and consistency. Strength, resilience and performance development require structured progression. A 12-week commitment ensures you fully engage in the process, build momentum and experience real, sustainable improvement.
