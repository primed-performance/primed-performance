FAQ

Common questions about 1:1 online coaching

How does online coaching even work?

It's simpler than it sounds. After your free discovery call and an initial assessment, I build your program and it lands in the TrainHeroic app on your phone. Every session is laid out for you with video demos, coaching cues, sets, reps and loads. You train when and where it suits you, log how it went, and I use that to adjust what comes next. On 1:1 coaching you can message me any time, and we catch up over video every month (fortnightly on Elite) to review your progress and plan your next block.

How do I know I'm doing the right technique when you're not there?

Every exercise in your program comes with a video demo and coaching cues, so you know what good looks like before you start. Then you film your sets on your phone and send them through. In return you get an in-depth video from me evaluating your movement, explaining any technical changes you need to make and how the movement should feel when you get it right. We also go through your technique in our video meetings, so we can make sure everything is going smoothly. If a movement isn't clicking, I'll swap it for one that suits your body better while we build it up. Video feedback and video meetings are part of Perform and Elite. On Build, use your monthly check-in to flag anything that doesn't feel right.

Do I need access to a gym?

Not necessarily. A fully equipped gym is ideal for long-term progression, but we know many people don't have consistent access to one. High-quality, effective programming can still be delivered with minimal equipment, so you keep developing strength, capacity and resilience regardless of location.

Is there a minimum commitment period?

Yes, we require a minimum 12-week commitment. Meaningful performance change takes time, consistency and commitment. We're focused on long-term development, not quick fixes or short-term transformations.

Why is strength & conditioning important for my sport?

It improves your ability to produce force, move efficiently, tolerate training loads and recover between sessions. Done properly, it enhances speed, power and endurance while reducing injury risk, so you train and compete at a higher level, more consistently.

I'm currently injured or working with a physio. Can I still start?

Yes. We take an integrated, team-based approach to your care. Open communication with your physiotherapist ensures your training supports rehabilitation, respects current limitations and progresses safely alongside your return to full performance.

What if it's not for me?

Every coaching option comes with a 2-week money-back guarantee. If it's not the right fit within your first 2 weeks, let me know and I'll refund you in full.

How do I get started?

Everyone starts with a free 15 minute discovery call to talk through your goals, training history and what you need. Complete the Expression of Interest form and Em will personally be in touch to book it in, or book the call directly.
